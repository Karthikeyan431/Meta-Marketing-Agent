# Settings, Roles and AI Policy UX
**Document ID:** UI-009 | Version 1.0

## Settings
- Workspace
- Team
- Roles
- Meta connections
- AI behavior
- Approval rules
- Spend limits
- Notifications
- Security

## AI Controls
Users with permission can configure:
- read-only vs execution capabilities
- approval thresholds
- autonomous optimization limits
- allowed operating hours
- action budgets
- emergency stop

## Safety
AI cannot modify the policies that govern its own authority.
