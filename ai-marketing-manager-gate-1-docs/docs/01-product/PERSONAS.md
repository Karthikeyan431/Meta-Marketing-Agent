# User Personas
**Document ID:** PROD-004 | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Business Owner
Goal: Understand and control advertising without needing platform expertise.

Needs:
- simple language
- business-level KPIs
- safe automation
- budget controls
- concise reports

## 2. Marketing Manager
Goal: Operate campaigns efficiently.

Needs:
- campaign detail
- diagnostics
- bulk operational workflows
- approvals
- reporting

## 3. Performance Marketer
Goal: Improve advertising efficiency.

Needs:
- granular metrics
- comparisons
- experimentation
- optimization controls
- transparent reasoning

## 4. Agency Operator
Goal: Manage multiple client workspaces safely.

Needs:
- workspace isolation
- RBAC
- account switching
- audit history
- client-ready reports
