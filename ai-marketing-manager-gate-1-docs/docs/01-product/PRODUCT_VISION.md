# AI Marketing Manager — Product Vision
**Document ID:** PROD-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Draft for Approval

## Vision
Make advertising operations conversational, goal-driven, measurable, and safely autonomous.

A user should be able to connect a Meta advertising ecosystem and manage it primarily through conversation:
- understand what is happening
- ask why it is happening
- request changes
- approve risky actions
- define business goals
- allow bounded optimization
- receive reports and explanations

## Product Promise
The platform turns natural-language marketing intent into controlled, observable advertising operations.

## Product Differentiators
1. Conversation-first campaign management.
2. Goal-driven optimization rather than isolated recommendations.
3. Controlled execution rather than AI-only advice.
4. Financial and operational guardrails.
5. Full auditability and explainability.
6. Multi-tenant foundation suitable for agencies and businesses.
7. Architecture extensible to additional advertising platforms later.

## Product North Star
A user can state a measurable advertising goal, understand the AI's plan, safely authorize required actions, and continuously monitor the resulting business outcome without manually navigating multiple advertising interfaces.
